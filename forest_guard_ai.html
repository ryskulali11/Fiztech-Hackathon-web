OUR PROJECT STORY & ENGINEERING EXPERIENCE

1. Problem & Innovation Journey

We built ForestGuard AI to replace delayed satellite imagery with instant acoustic detection. By deploying low-power edge micro-controllers directly in forest canopies, our system listens 24/7 and classifies sound patterns within milliseconds.

2. Key Learnings & Engineering Wins

During development, we mastered audio DSP filter design, spectrograph feature extraction, edge AI model optimization, and building responsive GIS web command centers for forestry teams.

SECTION 1 — THE CHALLENGE

FORESTS CAN'T CALL FOR HELP.

Traditional monitoring relies on satellite passes or tower lookouts, which often report incidents hours after damage has begun.

FOREST FIRES

A small fire can become a large-scale disaster before it is visually detected by optical sensors or satellite passes.

ILLEGAL LOGGING

Traditional ranger patrols cannot continuously cover vast forest territories, leaving vast zones unmonitored.

SLOW DETECTION

The earlier an abnormal event is detected, the more time emergency authorities have to respond effectively.
